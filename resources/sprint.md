All tracks are scored on one rubric.

## Judging

Three criteria, each scored 1–5:

- **Impact and innovation:** is it new, or a replication of recent work?
- **Execution:** methodology, validation, baselines, variance.
- **Presentation and clarity.**

## Submission

- **Required:** a PDF on the official template (linked on the Guidelines tab). At most 8 pages excluding references and appendices, a 150–250 word abstract, and authors and affiliations.
- **Required appendix:** "Limitations and Dual-Use / Ethical Considerations": what the result does and doesn't show, and how it could make multi-agent operations easier to carry out or harder to detect.
- **Optional:** a public repo and a 3–5 minute video.
- **Suggested structure:** introduction, related work, methodology (enough to replicate), results with variance and baselines, discussion.
- **Resubmitting:** submit again with the exact same title to replace the files.

## Rules

- **Prior work** is allowed, but only weekend work is judged. Say what existed before, and mark pre-sprint code as preexisting in the README and the report.
- **AI tools** are allowed. Made-up results or references disqualify a project.
- Each person can be on at most two projects.
